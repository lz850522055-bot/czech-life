# Mastery first: the Czech life simulation
[中文 below](#中文)

## The central promise we are working toward
**Your in-game level should correspond to what you can actually do in Czech outside the game.** A1, A2, B1, B2, C1 and C2 must eventually be evidence-based mastery milestones. The ambition is not to give a CEFR badge for enough points or story completion.

This is a product requirement and research/validation target, not a demonstrated result of the current alpha. CEFR describes language ability through can-do descriptors ([Council of Europe](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/level-descriptions)); an internal game label is not independent certification. C2 language mastery also does not automatically establish every practical, cultural or professional skill.

## What passing should require
- Comprehension of new listening/reading material at the target level, not a memorized quiz.
- Independent speech and writing; pronunciation, intelligibility and appropriate phrasing reviewed against defined rubrics.
- Interaction, clarification, repair and mediation when another person introduces an unexpected constraint.
- Transfer: solve a genuinely new scenario away from the practiced scene/wording.
- Retention: succeed again after a delay without rehearsing the same answer.
- Transparent skill evidence: show strengths/gaps separately; don't average a missing speaking skill away with quiz points.

Native Czech teachers/assessors should help define tasks, rubrics, reference performances and review. Test real learners against independent, level-appropriate assessments, including human evaluation. Publish results and limitations before claiming CEFR equivalence. Exact thresholds require evidence rather than invented pass percentages.

## Why reproduce a real city?
The 1:1 target covers actual roads/routes, buildings, infrastructure, landmarks and spatial relationships. Detailed 2D art remains the chosen presentation; it need not become a generic Google map or a heavy 3D model. Local geography and culture supply situations in which Czech is needed. Artistic simplification must be distinguished from verified geography.

Prague is the first region. The eventual national ambition is broad; it is not a claim that all buildings or all Czechia are already reconstructed.

## A life that makes the language necessary
Housing, groceries, transport, neighbors, appointments, work and notices. Business brings customers, suppliers, purchasing, stock, rents, wages, utilities, staffing and operating decisions. Customers are simulated characters in the single-player game; future networking may add human players.

Policy-grounded scenarios need jurisdiction, source URL, effective date, last verification, model assumptions and update history. Costs need location, period and a sourced/explicitly hypothetical value. Keep factual data apart from fictional choices. No real-world transactions/property rights are created by the game.

Culture and business are supporting systems. Their success criterion is whether they improve useful language and contextual competence, not merely how many features exist.

## Priority order
1. Define a small authentic competence target.
2. Build the playable scenario that needs it.
3. Test unprompted transfer and retention with learners.
4. Improve controls, art and feedback to support that learning.
5. Expand levels/locations/business systems with the same evidence discipline.
6. Add multiplayer after the core works.

## 中文

**第一目标是游戏能力与现实捷语能力对应。** 玩家通过A1、A2直到C2，不应只是“拿到游戏等级”，而应当真的具备对应的理解、表达和互动能力。这是要做成、要验证的验收目标，当前版本尚未证明这种等效关系。

通关要覆盖新材料的听读理解、独立说写、对话中的澄清与应变、陌生情境迁移，以及隔一段时间后的保持。应分别展示各项技能证据，由捷语老师和母语评审参与制定标准，并用独立测评检验真实玩家；不能让选择题积分掩盖不会说话，也不能用重复背过的题证明现实能力。具体分数门槛必须经过验证。

一比一城市、真实道路建筑、设施、人文和商业环境都服务于这个主目标。先从布拉格建立可信的空间与生活场景，再扩展捷克其他区域；保持选定的精细2D画风，准确地理与美术简化要分清。

生活与经营场景包括租房、买菜、交通、工作、邻里、客户、供货、库存、租金、人工、水电和经营成本。政策与价格资料要有适用范围、日期、来源和更新记录；游戏经营使用虚拟资产与模拟客户。联机与人类玩家互动属于后续路线。

验收最终看的是：玩家能否离开熟悉的题目和提示，在新的真实语言情境中独立完成事情。城市有多大、任务有多少，都是配套，不能替代这个结果。
