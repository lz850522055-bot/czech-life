# Roadmap: one excellent neighborhood first

## Current alpha

Sound gates; first district story; Chinese/Czech learning and saved progress; real-geography-backed 2D world with limited fine art; initial virtual business loop. The prior course includes a structure through C2; its presence is not proof of proficiency or native review.

## Acceptance milestones

1. **Playable neighborhood:** a new learner starts at zero, understands the sound task, uses a pattern in a fresh context and resumes later without lost progress. Observe real playtests; test counts do not measure fun or learning.
2. **Responsive exploration:** record input-to-frame, p50/p95 frame time and long frames on named desktop/phone devices, warm and cold caches. Profile fetch/decode/upload/draw separately.
3. **Language quality:** Czech native review of phonetics, natural dialogue and feedback; level-appropriate comprehension and production. Distinguish synthesized from natural speech.
4. **Prague expansion:** sourced roads/river/place anchors, consistent detailed 2D streets/interiors, Czech/Chinese labels and dated/provenanced local facts.
5. **Life/business:** meaningful shops, trade, neighborhood and fictional property systems that require the language, with consequences and recoverable saves.
6. **Access:** English UI, keyboard/touch parity, reduced motion, small-screen usability and deployment docs.
7. **Multiplayer research then implementation:** authoritative state, identity, abuse handling, moderation and affordable hosting. No multiplayer in this release.

Long-term: a Czech-life adventure from first sounds toward advanced expression, with collaborative play. No artificial fluency deadline. Expand beyond Prague after proving the local loop. Land small reviewed PRs with evidence; don't substitute a static concept picture for gameplay.
